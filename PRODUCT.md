# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Windows users who type a lot: developers, writers, support and ops staff. They want to dictate into any app without sending audio to a cloud. Many are privacy-conscious or work offline.

## Product purpose
Auralis is open-source, on-device push-to-talk dictation for Windows. Hold Ctrl+Space, speak, release; cleaned-up text is typed into the focused app. Everything runs locally on whisper.cpp. MIT licensed. A local HTTP API and a benchmark harness ship with it.

## Brand personality
Precise, calm, trustworthy, a little technical. An instrument, not a toy. Plain language, no hype.

## Anti-references
Generic AI SaaS landing pages, neon-on-black glow, gradient text, stock mic photography, fake testimonials and invented benchmarks.

## Design principles
1. The download is the first thing and the clearest thing.
2. Show the mechanism, not adjectives: hotkey, speech, text appearing.
3. Honest status. No invented numbers; say what is untested.
4. Privacy is a fact stated plainly: no cloud, no telemetry.
