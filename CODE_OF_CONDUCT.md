# Code of Conduct

We want Auralis to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and contributions that violate this code, and may ban people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately through the email on their GitHub profile.

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
