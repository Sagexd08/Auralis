Getting Auralis ready.

Auralis turns speech into text on this PC. It needs a speech model first. It downloads once and stays here.

Starting…

No audio or text ever leaves your computer.

Hold. Speak. Release.

Two shortcuts work in any app.

Push to talk
Hold while you speak, let go and the text appears.
Hands-free
Tap to start, tap again to stop.

Another app using one of these?

Try it here.

Click the box, hold , say a sentence, then let go.

Ready

Nothing typed? Check that Windows allows microphone access for desktop apps (Settings, Privacy, Microphone), then try again.

You’re set.

Auralis lives in your system tray, bottom right.

  • Fix a mistake out loud. Say “actually, change Rahul to Rohan” within 45 seconds and Auralis retypes the sentence.
  • Choose how much it rewrites. Settings has raw, clean, polished and developer modes.
  • Private by design. Transcription runs on this PC with no cloud and no telemetry.

Reopen this guide anytime from the tray menu.
